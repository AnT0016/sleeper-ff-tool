## Calibration — realized mean by predicted decile

Each cell is the realized mean custom points of the rows in that predicted decile (d1 = lowest predicted, d10 = highest). Realized points climbing monotonically across the deciles is the ordering property; `gap` = mean |predicted − realized| across deciles is the level mismatch.

**Read `gap` next to `ordered`.** A baseline that predicts the position mean scores a near-zero gap *by construction* — it is the mean — while its deciles are noise. That is the case for LaggedExpectedPoints/DEF, LaggedExpectedPoints/K below, whose small gaps are the arithmetic of a flat prediction rather than evidence of calibration.
